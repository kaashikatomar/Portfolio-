01 — ABOUTA work in becoming

A LITTLE ABOUT ME

Curious by nature.
Becoming by choice.

I'm a first-year college student with a creative mindset, a curious nature, and a desire to keep growing.

I can be a little shy when I first meet someone, but once a connection begins, my more expressive side comes through. I enjoy finding my own way to connect with people, learning to handle things independently, and welcoming experiences that challenge me.

Creativity, independence, ambition, and the freedom to live life on my own terms matter to me. Through Bharatanatyam, fashion, personal expression, and a growing interest in coding, I'm discovering new sides of myself.

I hope to build something of my own, create financial independence, make my parents proud, and accomplish things that reflect the effort and belief I put into my dreams.

Still learning. Always becoming.
